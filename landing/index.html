Fermentation Monitor Early access

Know what your fermenter is doing, without opening it.

A small WiFi sensor reports your ferment's temperature every 15 minutes and emails you when it drifts outside the range you set, when its sensor stops reading, or when its battery runs low, so a cold night or a hot afternoon doesn't wreck your kombucha, beer, or wine before you notice.

Two versions: a hub with a temperature probe, or a sealed float that reads temperature and gravity from inside the vessel.

It's not built for sale yet. We're gauging interest before committing to a batch.

How it works

  1. Put it in

    The hub's waterproof probe goes on or into your fermenter; the float drops straight into the liquid. Both run on a rechargeable battery.

  2. It reports

    It wakes every 15 minutes (you can change that), sends a reading over your home WiFi, and sleeps again. Setup is picking your network from your phone. No code to edit.

  3. You get warned

    An email when temperature stays outside your range for several readings in a row, not on a single noisy blip, and when the sensor or battery needs attention.
