🧪 Fermentation Monitor Get early access

Stop losing batches to temperature swings.

A small WiFi sensor clips onto your fermenter and watches the temperature around the clock — so a cold night or a hot afternoon doesn't wreck your kombucha, beer, or wine before you notice.

It's not built for sale yet — we're gauging interest before committing to a batch.

How it works

1

Clip it on

A waterproof probe straps to your fermenter or airlock. Runs weeks on a single battery charge.

2

It watches

Readings every few minutes over your home WiFi — no hub, no app pairing dance.

3

You get warned

An email the moment temperature drifts outside your range for real, not on a single noisy blip.
